    Annelik Hikayesi — "The Story of Motherhood" — is a content platform that accompanies the whole of a mother's journey, from planning a pregnancy through week-by-week tracking and month-by-month infant development to parenting guides. For the platform we built a complete brand world spanning logo, website, mobile app and expert-led video production.

    The sensitivity here was particular: the subject is both emotional and dense with information. The brand had to feel warm and personal while carrying enough seriousness for an expectant mother to trust what she reads. Every design decision was built on that balance.

  approach:
    heading: What We Did
    text: |-
      We started with the hand-lettered logo: the heart worked into it and the flowing line carry the feeling of motherhood, while a purple palette separates the platform from the generic pink-and-blue cliché of the category.

      We carried that identity into the website first. The deep pool of content — pregnancy weeks, month-by-month development, parenting guides — was organised through tools and category structures a mother can navigate according to the stage she is at.

      We then extended the same language into the mobile app, the social media content and video production. The warm tone is held in every detail, down to the crying-baby illustration on the 404 page.
  sections:
    - heading: Logo & Brand Identity
      text: |-
        We designed a hand-lettered logotype for the brand; the heart resting over the syllables of the name forms its emotional centre.

        We adapted the identity to stickers, enamel pins, patterns and the app icon, turning it into a consistent set that runs from digital into physical products. The simplified heart in the app icon keeps the logo recognisable at small sizes.
      images:

    - heading: Website & Mobile App
      text: |-
        We designed a platform that opens on a clear promise: honest stories and guidance on motherhood. On the homepage, a carousel of tools alongside pregnancy planning, week-by-week pregnancy and month-by-month development sections guides the mother by the stage she is at.

        We turned weekly pregnancy tracking into a visual timeline; a mother selects her own week and reaches that period's content directly. In the baby and parenting categories, content lists were made scannable through a card structure.

        A dedicated section on the site introduces the mobile app, and the brand's language was carried into every screen, from the contact page to the 404.

    - heading: Video & Photography
      text: |-
        We produced video and photography for the brand in collaboration with psychologists and specialists in their fields.

        We split the content into two lines. The first is expert opinion: series such as "A Mother's Unspoken Questions", "Is Motherhood Half Madness?", "Mother-in-Law Wars" and "A Healthy Marriage" that address the emotional weight a mother carries.

        The second is practical guidance from the field — decisions a mother actually faces, such as furnishing a nursery. Informative content builds trust; practical content puts the brand into everyday life. Both accumulate in the same archive.
