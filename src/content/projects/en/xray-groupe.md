We built a complete communication structure around the brand — website, social media, video editing and corporate catalogue.

  approach:
    heading: What We Did
    text: |-
      We built the whole system on a single question: where does the evidence of this company's work actually live?

      The answer was on site. We took the raw footage coming back from the field and cut it into the brand's language; the website, the social feed and the catalogue were then built on that same material. A construction brand told through stock images and renders is indistinguishable from one that does no work at all — the difference is made by a frame from your own site.

      The second decision was about positioning. Xray Groupe is a Turkish firm working in Congo: the communication had to earn trust in the local market and speak to international investors at the same time.
  sections:
    - heading: Website
      text: |-
        We treated the site as an archive of evidence rather than a list of services. Infrastructure works, engineering services and residential developments are separated by a clear hierarchy, each with its own detail page.

        Projects such as Villa Orange and Lukusa Business Center open individually: location, project type, start and completion dates, and current status on a single screen. The investor is not reading an answer to "could you do this?" — they are looking at finished work.

        A site gallery supports the structure with photography, floor plans and location tabs.

    - heading: Social Media
      text: |-
        We split the feed into three lines: project announcements, the brand's vision and mission, and content tied to the national calendar.

        The third is the one construction brands usually skip. Posts marking Congo's independence day or commemorating Patrice Lumumba move the brand from being a foreign company that works in the country to an institution that follows what happens there.

        Both post and story formats are bound to the same visual language as the corporate identity.

    - heading: Video Editing
      text: |-
        We took the raw footage coming in from the site and turned it into the brand's broadcast language: edit, colour, pacing and subtitles.

        The footage is not decoration here, it is the foundation of the system: the project pages on the website, the social feed and the corporate catalogue all draw on the same material.

        The edit serves three separate jobs: a drone view of a finished building, a site film following work in progress, and a year-in-review film pulling the year's projects together.

        We designed the corporate catalogue: a reference document that accompanies sales and business development meetings, covering both the company's services and the details of completed projects.
