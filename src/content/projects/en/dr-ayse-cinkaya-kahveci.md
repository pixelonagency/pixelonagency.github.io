  approach:
    heading: What We Did
    text: |-
      We began by building the monogram logo derived from the physician's initials, along with the corporate identity; a palette based on powder pink and white carries the elegance the aesthetic dermatology field expects without disturbing medical seriousness.

      On the website we separated two different patient expectations: a visitor looking for an aesthetic treatment and a patient arriving with a dermatological complaint are not searching for the same content. We placed non-surgical aesthetic treatments and general dermatology conditions into separate category architectures; every treatment received its own detail page.

      With the Health Guide section we built the SEO side through content. Social media, meanwhile, became an information feed answering directly the followers curious about the treatments.
  sections:
    - heading: Website
      text: |-
        We designed a clinic site gathering non-surgical aesthetic treatments — fillers, botox, mesotherapy, laser hair removal, PRP, pigmentation treatment — within their own category, each with a detail page. On the filler page we made application areas such as nose, lips, under-eye, chin tip and cheekbone individually selectable.

        We placed the general dermatology side into a separate architecture: by listing conditions such as eczema, psoriasis, vitiligo, urticaria, seborrhoeic dermatitis and nail disorders in a visual grid, we let a patient arriving with a complaint find their own condition quickly.

        We built the trust layer through the physician's background, clinic photography, patient testimonials and FDA/CE device certifications. We closed every page with an appointment call, and the site with a contact block containing working hours, phone and map.
