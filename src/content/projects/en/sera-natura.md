  approach:
    heading: What We Did
    text: |-
      We started with the packaging, because in natural products the first decision about trust is made at the shelf. On top of the amber glass bottle and gold cap, we built a label system in which every product carries its own botanical illustration.

      We designed the system to work across the whole range rather than on a single product: the same layout, the same typography, the same badge placement — only the plant illustration and the colour accent change. Adding a new product therefore does not mean redesigning anything.

      We carried the same language into social media, advertising and email. A natural products buyer does not purchase before being convinced, so on every channel we explained what the product is for first and asked for the sale second. Against the pre-campaign monthly baseline, the best month came in 87% higher.
  sections:
    - heading: Packaging Design
      text: |-
        We designed a label system for the essential oil range that works across every product. On each one — Niaouli, Cypress, Orange Peel — the brand crest sits at the centre, the product's own botanical illustration beside it, and a "100% Pure" badge in the corner.

        On the label we placed the Latin name, the volume and the steam distillation method in a readable hierarchy — giving the natural products buyer the transparency they look for without turning it into visual clutter.

        We tested the system across colour and ground variations, confirming it stays equally legible on amber glass and on both light and dark presentation backgrounds.

      text: |-
        For a brand with more than twenty products we produced a continuous content stream in post and story formats.

        We pushed the content past product promotion: how MCT oil supports energy, where lavender is used, what tamanu oil does for skin, which natural supports belong in a holiday bag. Every piece carries the brand crest and web address, reinforcing recognition.

    - heading: UGC & Video
      text: |-
        We produced videos in UGC format: not studio set-ups, but real users speaking in their own words, in their own homes.

        In the natural products category that format pays, because the buyer is not watching the product — they are watching the person using it. The scent of Udi Hindi oil, Mite-End in use around the house, Nioli oil applied to skin: all things shown rather than described.

        The videos ran in the organic feed and in advertising, where they passed a million views.

    - heading: Email Marketing
      text: |-
        We set up the email marketing channel so the brand could reach its own customer list, and produced the campaign designs.

        In e-commerce the cheapest sale comes from a customer who has already bought from you — you do not have to find them again through advertising. Seasonal campaigns such as "Buy One, Get One Free" were built on that logic: product imagery, the campaign terms, and a single clear call.

        The design language matches the packaging: the same crest, the same botanical warmth. The brand seen in the inbox looks like the brand on the shelf.
