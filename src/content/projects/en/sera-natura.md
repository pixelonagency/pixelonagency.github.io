  approach:
    heading: What We Did
    text: |-
      We started with packaging, because in the natural products category the first decision of trust is made at the shelf. On top of the amber glass bottle and gold cap, we built a label system carrying each product's own botanical illustration.

      We designed the system to work not on a single product but across the entire range: the same layout, the same typography, the same badge arrangement — only the plant illustration and colour accent change. When a new product joins the range, the design does not have to be rebuilt.

      We carried the same language into social media and production. We supported product introductions not only with imagery but with informational content explaining what each oil is used for; through influencer collaborations we gave the brand real stories of use.
  sections:
    - heading: Packaging Design
      text: |-
        For the essential oil series we designed a label system that works across the whole range. On every product — Nioli, Servi, Orange Peel — the brand crest sits at the centre, the product's own botanical illustration beside it, and the "100% Pure" badge in the corner.

        On the labels we positioned information such as the product's Latin name, volume and steam distillation method in a readable hierarchy — giving the content transparency natural product buyers look for without turning it into visual clutter.

        We tested the system across different colour and background variations, confirming it holds the same legibility on amber glass and on both light and dark presentation backgrounds.
