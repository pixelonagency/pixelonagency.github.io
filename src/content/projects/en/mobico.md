  approach:
    heading: What We Did
    text: |-
      We built the emblem on the idea of combining different objects; that combination points to the meeting of customer and management — precisely what Mobico does. The emblem also carries the "M", the first letter of the brand name.

      In the logotype we used a font with modern, futuristic lines to create an aesthetic and technological appearance. The navy and green palette balances the corporate seriousness of the software category with energy.

      We did not leave the identity as a logo alone: from business cards and letterheads to presentation templates, t-shirts and building signage, we brought every touchpoint the brand would need into the system.
  sections:
    - heading: Logo Design
      text: |-
        The emblem was designed around the theme of combining different objects, pointing to the meeting of customer and management. The same structure also contains the "M", the first letter of the brand name.

        The font used in the logotype has modern and futuristic lines, creating an aesthetic and technological appearance. By pairing the brand with the slogan "Know Your Customer, Grow Your Business!", we gave the identity a verbal counterpart as well.

        We tested the logo at different scales and on different grounds: LED screen, building signage, envelope seal, t-shirt and app icon — it holds the same recognisability on green and on navy.

    - heading: Corporate Identity
      text: |-
        We prepared an identity set for the brand consisting of letterhead, business cards and a corporate presentation template.

        We built the presentation template to be usable without needing a designer: we created ready layouts for the cover, section title, bulleted content, text-heavy page and closing. By defining the typographic hierarchy point by point through the Axiforma family, we let the team reproduce the template without breaking it.

        By repeating the linear angular motif derived from the emblem across every piece, we established visual continuity between documents.

By showing the relevant screen in every piece, we made the software tangible.
