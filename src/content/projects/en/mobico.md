  approach:
    heading: What We Did
    text: |-
      Software brands share one dead end: because the product is abstract, the brand becomes abstract too. To describe a service nobody can see, most brands fall back on generic technology imagery — and end up indistinguishable from each other.

      Our decision here was to make the emblem do the work. Whatever the company does, the mark should say it — without needing a paragraph of explanation.

      The second decision concerned the form of the delivery. We handed over the identity not as a logo file but as a system the team can extend on its own: from the presentation template to the building signage, every touchpoint the brand will need, under rules.
  sections:
    - heading: Logo Design
      text: |-
        The emblem is built on the idea of separate objects joining: the customer and the management coming together — which is exactly what Mobico does. The same form also holds the "M" of the brand name, so the mark carries both the meaning and the name.

        For the logotype we used a face with modern, futuristic lines; the navy-and-green palette balances the corporate seriousness the software category expects with some energy. Pairing the identity with the line "Know your customer, grow your business" gave the visual answer a verbal one alongside it.

        We tested the logo across scales and grounds: LED screen, building signage, envelope seal, T-shirt and app icon — it holds its recognisability on green and on navy alike.
      images:

    - heading: Corporate Identity & Presentation System
      text: |-
        We prepared an identity set of letterhead, business cards and a corporate presentation template.

        The presentation template was built to be used without a designer: ready-made layouts for a cover, a section title, a bulleted page, a text-heavy page and a closing slide. Defining the typographic hierarchy point by point through the Axiforma family lets the team reproduce the template without breaking it.

        The linear angular motif derived from the emblem repeats across every piece, creating visual continuity between documents.

      text: |-
        For a brand building CRM infrastructure we produced designs consistent with the corporate colours and type family.

Every piece shows the relevant screen, making the software tangible.
