      text: |-
        The emblem was designed by combining a heart figure with the letter "B"; through the heart figure, the meaning of beauty was intended. The green tones chosen for the logo represent the brand's calm, reassuring and refined understanding of aesthetics — a colour balance that translates the surgeon's sensitivity and meticulousness into a visual language.

        By placing the subtitle "Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery Specialist" beneath the handwritten surname, we made the specialisation an inseparable part of the identity.

        We applied the identity to the touchpoints of the physician's real working environment: clinic signage, surgical masks, scrubs, business cards and digital icon variations. By separating the emblem so it works on its own, we also gave the brand an independent mark for small-scale use.

    - heading: Social Media
      text: |-
        We run personal social media management for the surgeon, having built a design language specific to his corporate identity.

        We built the content around the questions patients genuinely wonder about: do aesthetic operations leave scars, does botox freeze your expression, who is suitable for breast reduction surgery. Treatments such as the mommy makeover were addressed through process explanation rather than promises of results.

        In the visual language we chose a soft and natural tone; by avoiding exaggerated before-and-after constructions we preserved the brand's refined stance. Special-day posts were brought into the feed in the same calm language.
