For this platform we built a holistic brand world spanning logo, website, mobile app presentation and expert-led video production.

  approach:
    heading: What We Did
    text: |-
      We began with the brand's handwritten logo: the heart figure within it and its flowing lines carry the emotion of motherhood, while the purple palette sets the platform apart from the generic pink-and-blue cliché of the category.

      We carried that identity into the website first. We organised a deep content pool — pregnancy weeks, month-by-month baby development, parenting guides — into tools and category structures a mother can navigate according to the stage she is in.

      We then extended the same language into the mobile app, social media content and video production. Down to the crying-baby illustration on the 404 page, every detail preserves the brand's warm tone.
  sections:
    - heading: Logo & Brand Identity
      text: |-
        We designed a handwritten logotype for the brand; the heart figure sitting above the syllables forms its emotional centre.

        By adapting the identity to different touchpoints — stickers, pins, patterns and the app icon — we turned it into a consistent set reaching from digital into physical products. The simplified heart in the app icon keeps the logo recognisable at small scale.
      images:

    - heading: Website & Mobile App
      text: |-
        We designed a platform opening with the promise of "intimate stories and guiding content about motherhood". On the home page, a tools carousel and sections for pregnancy planning, week-by-week pregnancy and month-by-month baby development guide mothers according to their stage.

        We turned weekly pregnancy tracking into a visual timeline; a mother can select her own week and reach the content for that period directly. In the baby and parenting categories, content lists were made scannable through a card structure.

        We built a dedicated section on the site introducing the mobile app, and carried the brand's language into every screen — from the contact page to the 404.
