  approach:
    heading: What We Did
    text: |-
      We started with Touch Consulting at the very beginning of the brand: we designed the logo that would carry its name, its colour and its corporate identity. The rising form in the upper right of the "T" symbolises the brand's promise of growth, while the blue and navy palette refers to the sense of trust the consultancy sector expects.

      We then designed the digital home to carry that identity — a website that separates the three service areas without blurring them, gives each its own in-depth page, and closes every page with a contact form.

      Finally we fed social media and advertising with the same visual language, so that wherever a prospective client encountered the brand, they saw the same consistency.
  sections:
    - heading: Logo & Corporate Identity
      text: |-
        Starting from the logo, we prepared a complete corporate identity set for the brand: business cards, notepads, lanyards and icon variations for use across digital channels.

        The construction of the logo tells the story of the business: the rise in the upper right of the "T" symbolises growth, while the blue and navy pairing refers to the "business" field and to trust. We delivered the identity with variations that work on both dark and light backgrounds.
      images:

    - heading: Website
      text: |-
        We designed a corporate website that separates the brand's three service areas — investment incentives, health tourism incentives and KOSGEB incentives — in a clear hierarchy. The home page introduces the services, while each area goes deeper on its own detail page.

        On the health tourism incentives page we structured every support item into an individually expandable layout, from advertising and promotion support to patient travel support, agency commission and employment support. We built the Investment Incentive Certificate page on the same logic.

        By closing each page with a "Free Online Consultation" call and a direct form block, we turned the site from a promotional showcase into a structure that collects enquiries. The working strategy section makes the steps of the process transparent.

Social media therefore became not only a brand visibility channel but a source of information the target audience genuinely follows.
