  approach:
    heading: What We Did
    text: |-
      Nobody buys incentive consultancy on feeling. A business asks one thing: is there a support programme that fits me, how much is it, and how do I get it? The regulation is already complicated enough — the brand's job is not to make it worse.

      So we built the whole system around reduction. The three service lines — investment incentives, health tourism incentives, KOSGEB grants — were kept strictly apart, and every individual support item was made openable on its own.

      The second decision was about what the site is for. Consultancy sites are usually shop windows: they explain, they introduce, they send you to a contact page. Here every page closes with a form block — the site does not introduce, it collects enquiries.
  sections:
    - heading: Logo & Corporate Identity
      text: |-
        We built the brand from scratch. The emblem rests on a form rising out of the top-right corner of the letter "T" — which is exactly what incentive consultancy promises: to carry a business up from where it stands.

        The blue-and-navy palette is a deliberate choice. Consultancy touches a client's money and state regulation at the same time; the colour here has to carry trust, not excitement.

        We applied the identity to the surfaces the brand actually uses: business cards, notepads, lanyards and digital icon variants. The emblem was drawn to work on both dark and light grounds, and still reads on its own at small sizes.

    - heading: Website
      text: |-
        We designed a corporate site separating the brand's three service lines — investment incentives, health tourism incentives and KOSGEB grants — through a clear hierarchy. The homepage introduces them; each line then goes deep on its own page.

        On the health tourism incentives page we placed every support item into an individually expandable structure: advertising and promotion support, patient travel support, agency commission, employment support. The Investment Incentive Certificate page follows the same logic.

        Closing every page with a "Free Online Consultation" call and a direct form block turned the site from a brochure into a structure that gathers enquiries. A working-strategy section makes the steps of the process transparent.

    - heading: Social Media
      text: |-
        For a brand working across three service lines we produce a continuous content stream in post, story and reels formats.

        The content follows what actually changes in the regulation: certification support for exporters, dedicated programmes for women entrepreneurs, capacity-building application windows, the advantages of an investment incentive certificate. That turns the feed from a visibility exercise into a source the audience genuinely follows.

        We film the videos ourselves. Each reel takes a single support item and answers one question — is this any use to you? Complication insurance support, employment support for staff, how incentives apply to influencer partnerships. Nobody reads the regulation; explained out loud, they watch it.

        Every video is subtitled. Anyone watching with the sound off can still follow.
