  approach:
    heading: What We Did
    text: |-
      For a Turkish specialist in the German market, the greatest advantage and the greatest risk sit in the same place: the community. The Turkish community adopts the brand quickly, but if communication stops there the brand stays boxed in as a neighbourhood practice.

      So we built the system in two layers. The Turkish side carries trust and familiarity; the German side positions the brand as a local health service — the advertising is entirely in German, gathered around one claim: "Das ist keine Diät!"

      The third decision was geographic. Instead of one campaign for all of Germany, we set up a separate campaign for each service point; Castrop, Cologne and Frankfurt each reach their own surroundings with their own advertising. As the clinic count grew to three, the communication structure grew with it.

      text: |-
        We designed a multilingual site bringing nutrition consultancy and device-assisted treatments — Vanquish Me, Exilis Ultra 360, Emsculpt NEO, Tixel, Andullation — under one roof. In the treatments section each application is listed on its own visual card, so the visitor compares and chooses.

        We added a product sales layer: detox packages, thermal products, teas and measurement-and-analysis services, each in its own category. This layer is not a shop window but the continuation of the consultancy — the client following the plan gets what they need in the same place.

        Trust is built on local proof: real interior photography of all three service points, Google and Trustpilot reviews, and a "Who Are We?" section introducing every team member. Each location's contact details and opening hours sit in their own tab.

        We built the advertising on video and split it by city. The campaigns also run into the Netherlands — a meaningful extension for the Turkish community near the border.

        Over two years the content was viewed 18 million times: 11.2 million organically, 6.7 million through advertising. Link clicks rose 335% to 533,000, and profile visits reached 942,000.

    - heading: From First Consultation to Sale
      text: |-
        The brand's revenue does not come from a single service but from four steps that follow one another. We built the digital side around that.

        It all begins with a first consultation: measurements are taken, goals are discussed. A personalised nutrition plan follows. Clients who want to accelerate the process add device-assisted treatments — Emsculpt NEO, Tixel, Exilis Ultra 360. The final link is product: teas, coffees and thermal clothing.

        The job of advertising is to feed the first link in that chain. Campaigns target the consultation, not the sale, because the rest of the chain already starts there. Over three years we raised the number of new first consultations by 30%.

        This is exactly why CRM implementation sits in the service list: in a structure that cannot track which step a client is on, nobody reaches the fourth link.
