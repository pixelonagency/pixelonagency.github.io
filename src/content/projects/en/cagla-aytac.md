  approach:
    heading: What We Did
    text: |-
      We opened the site with the question "Are you ready to change together?" — positioning the service not as a diet imposed on you but as a process walked together.

      Alongside nutrition consultancy we added device-assisted treatments such as Vanquish Me, Evolis Ultra 360 and Emsculpt, introducing each on its own visual card so visitors could compare.

      We built brand trust through local proof: real interior photography of the service points, Google and Trustpilot reviews, a "Who Are We?" section introducing every team member, and the GmbH corporate identity. We also reduced the process to three steps to make starting easier.
  sections:
    - heading: Website
      text: |-
        We designed a multilingual site presenting nutrition consultancy and body shaping treatments under one roof. In the treatments section each application is listed on its own visual card, letting visitors compare and choose the method that suits them.

        We added a product sales layer to the site: detox packages, thermal products, teas and measurement-and-analysis services are listed with their prices in a showcase divided into categories. A visitor not taking the consultancy service can therefore make first contact with the brand through a product.

        We supported corporate trust with the team introduction on the About page, certifications, interior photography of the service points and customer reviews. Each service point's contact details and working hours were positioned in their own tabs.

      text: |-
        We prepared a design system for the brand in post and story formats, explaining all services in a plain and understandable language.

        We built the content directly in German for the German market and gathered it around the brand's core claim: "Das ist keine Diät!" — this is not a diet. We foregrounded differentiating messages such as a personalised nutrition system, independence from time and place through online consultation, and a medically device-assisted process.

        By preserving the brand logo and a consistent green-and-white palette in every visual, we created a visual signature recognised instantly in the feed.
