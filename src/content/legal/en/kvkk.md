## 1. Data Controller

For personal data processed through this website (pixelon.com.tr), the data controller is **Mehmet Fatih Dayan** ("Pixelon", "we"), operating under the "Pixelon" brand at Sahrayıcedit Mah. Şafak Sok. No:1, Kadıköy / İstanbul, Türkiye.

Contact: [info@pixelon.com.tr](mailto:info@pixelon.com.tr) · +90 506 522 90 34

## 2. Which Personal Data Do We Process?

We only process data you share with us through this website or data that arises technically while the site operates:

- **Identity and contact data:** full name, e-mail address, phone number; and, if you share them in the contact form, your company/brand name and website address.
- **Request content:** the service you are interested in, estimated project budget, your message; and, on the free analysis form, the areas you would like analysed.
- **Technical and security data:** IP address and request logs kept by the hosting infrastructure, and the `pixelon-consent` cookie that stores your cookie preference.

The website has no membership, payment, newsletter or job application forms; no such data is collected.

## 3. Purposes of Processing

- Responding to requests submitted through the contact and free analysis forms,
- Evaluating your request and preparing a tailored proposal,
- Carrying out the communication you initiate,
- Operating the website securely and correctly,
- Remembering your cookie preference.

## 4. Method of Collection

Your personal data is collected through the forms on this website, when you e-mail or call us or reach us via WhatsApp, and automatically through the technical operation of the site. When you click WhatsApp or social media links, the rules of the relevant platform apply.

## 5. Legal Grounds

- Contact and free analysis forms: processing is necessary, at your request, to prepare a proposal for you and to take steps directly related to the establishment of a contract (Article 5/2-c of the KVKK).
- Security logs and the consent cookie: our legitimate interest in operating the website securely and lawfully, provided that your fundamental rights and freedoms are not harmed (Article 5/2-f of the KVKK).

- Analytics measurement (Google Analytics 4): the permission you give to the Analytics category in the cookie preference panel — without it, Analytics cookies are not used and no cookie-based measurement takes place (as part of Google's Consent Mode infrastructure, cookieless, non-identifying consent signals may still be sent to Google); you can withdraw your permission at any time.

Beyond these, we do not currently carry out any processing that requires explicit consent (such as marketing communications); if we do in the future, your consent will be requested separately and explicitly.

## 6. Transfers

Form submissions are delivered to our mailbox through **Web3Forms**, the form relay service we use, and are read only by Pixelon. Your data is not sold to third parties or shared for marketing purposes.

## 7. International Transfers

The servers of our hosting provider (GitHub Pages), our form relay service (Web3Forms) and the Google components used on the site (Google Tag Manager, Google Fonts and Google Analytics 4 — cookie-based measurement depends on your Analytics permission) may be located outside Türkiye. The data listed above may therefore be processed abroad, limited strictly to these operational purposes.

## 8. Retention

If your proposal or contact request does not turn into a client relationship, the related form and e-mail records are deleted within 2 years at the latest after the request is concluded. If a client relationship is established, the related records may be kept for as long as required under contractual, accounting and other applicable statutory retention obligations. Technical data such as security logs and the consent cookie preference are not subject to this period and are kept only as long as necessary for their own purposes.

## 9. Your Rights (Article 11 of the KVKK)

You have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed; to request information if it has been; to learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used accordingly; to know the third parties to whom it is transferred; to request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data; to request erasure or destruction; to request that these actions be notified to third-party recipients; to object to a result arising against you from analysis made exclusively by automated systems; and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

## 10. How to Apply

You can submit requests regarding your rights by e-mailing [info@pixelon.com.tr](mailto:info@pixelon.com.tr) or in writing to Sahrayıcedit Mah. Şafak Sok. No:1, Kadıköy / İstanbul. Applications are concluded as soon as possible and within the statutory periods.
