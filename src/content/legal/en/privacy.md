## 1. Scope

This policy applies to the pixelon.com.tr website operated by Mehmet Fatih Dayan under the "Pixelon" brand ("Pixelon"). The legally required disclosure on personal data is the [Personal Data Processing Notice](/en/personal-data-processing-notice); this page summarises our practices in a broader, more readable form.

## 2. What We Collect

- **When you share it:** full name, e-mail, phone, company/brand, website, the service you are interested in, budget range and your message — through the contact and free analysis forms.
- **Automatically:** request logs kept by the hosting infrastructure while serving the site (including IP address) and the `pixelon-consent` cookie storing your cookie preference.

There is no membership system, payment infrastructure, newsletter signup or job application form on this website; no such data is collected.

## 3. Why We Use It

Only to respond to your request, prepare a proposal, carry out the communication you initiate and run the website securely. Your information is not sold to third parties and is not shared for advertising purposes.

## 4. Forms

Form submissions are relayed to our mailbox through **Web3Forms** and are read only by Pixelon. Short notices under the forms inform you before you submit.

## 5. Cookies and Measurement

Cookie preferences are managed with the open-source **Klaro** consent manager, served from our own domain. Analytics and marketing categories are **off by default**, and **no analytics or advertising measurement service is currently active** on the site. Google Tag Manager is loaded only as tag-management infrastructure and operates with your consent signals (Google Consent Mode v2). See the [Cookie Policy](/en/cookie-policy) for the current cookie list.

## 6. Third-Party Services

The website relies on the following providers:

- **GitHub Pages** — website hosting,
- **Web3Forms** — form relay,
- **Google** — Tag Manager infrastructure and web fonts.

Their servers may be located outside Türkiye; their privacy policies are their own and are not covered by this policy.

## 7. Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect your information, and access is limited to those who need it for the work. No internet transmission can be guaranteed absolutely secure; please let us know if you notice anything suspicious.

## 8. Retention

If your proposal or contact request does not turn into a client relationship, the related form and e-mail records are deleted within 2 years at the latest after the request is concluded. If a client relationship is established, the related records may be kept for as long as required under contractual, accounting and other applicable statutory retention obligations. Other data categories are kept only as long as necessary for their own purposes.

## 9. Your Rights

Your rights under the KVKK and how to exercise them are described in the [Personal Data Processing Notice](/en/personal-data-processing-notice). For questions, write to [info@pixelon.com.tr](mailto:info@pixelon.com.tr).

## 10. External Links

The site links to third-party platforms such as WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook. When you follow those links, the privacy rules of the relevant platform apply.

## 11. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are aimed at adults (businesses and professionals); we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## 12. Changes

This policy is updated when our data practices change. The "Last Updated" date at the top reflects genuine content changes.
